# Formation trace — `{ PEEL. SLEEP .LEEP }`

Use this reference when the path by which a question, hypothesis, reading, or
claim formed is materially useful and must remain distinguishable from the
evidence that later bears on it.

The protocol preserves research formation without granting formation history
source authority.

## Constitutional distinctions

```text
surface != formation history
discovery path != evidence path
breadcrumb != evidence
toast-ghost != evidence
toast-ghost != genealogy
replay match != historical identity
```

A coincidence, joke, dream, typo, visual resemblance, personal association,
metaphor, or theological intuition may legitimately choose the next door to
inspect. That grants **search motive**, not evidentiary weight.

> **Serendipity may choose the door. Evidence decides what can walk through it.**

## BREADCRUMBS

A `breadcrumb` is an attributable transition that actually occurred in the
formation of the inquiry.

Examples:

- observation -> question;
- phrase -> search query;
- source finding -> corrected hypothesis;
- calculation -> new discriminator;
- analogy -> bounded comparison.

A breadcrumb should preserve, when material:

```text
from_state
to_state
move
actor_or_producer
time_or_order
role
reason
source_or_tool_boundary
```

Useful `role` values include:

- `motive`
- `evidence`
- `counterevidence`
- `inference`
- `analogy`
- `coincidence`
- `wordplay`
- `discriminator`

A breadcrumb with `role: motive`, `coincidence`, or `wordplay` explains why a
search happened. It does not support the result found by that search unless a
separate evidence path establishes support.

## TOAST-GHOSTS

A `toast-ghost` is an attributable residue of formation that is no longer the
active surface but still helps explain how the active surface acquired its
shape.

Examples:

- a killed hypothesis whose failure redirected the search;
- an abandoned ordering;
- a discarded interpretation;
- a compression loss;
- a coincidence that caused discovery but proves nothing;
- an unexplained replay delta;
- a branch that failed to become the present result but left consequences.

A toast-ghost is not automatically an error. It is also not evidence merely
because it persisted.

Preserve enough to answer:

```text
what_was_here
why_it_left_the_active_path
what_consequence_remains
whether_it_can_be_revisited
```

> **Formation history preserves the road and the roadside.**

## PEEL. — expose formation backward

`PEEL.` walks a present surface backward into attributable formation without
destroying the surface.

Given a present state `X`, ask:

> What had to happen for `X` to have this shape, and what relevant residue was
> left beside the winning path?

A PEEL may emit:

```text
surface
breadcrumbs[]
toast_ghosts[]
losses[]
ambiguities[]
branches_not_taken[]
```

The terminal period is mnemonic: expose the layers, then **stop**. Do not let
the first decomposition immediately become the preferred explanation.

## +S / SLEEP — bounded suspension

`S` means **SUSPENSION**.

SLEEP holds **one to three** live formulations in a self-correcting equilibrium
before forward replay.

```text
SUSPEND {
  H1
  H2
  H3
}
```

One is enough. Three is the cap, not a quota.

Equilibrium does **not** mean equal probability or equal confidence. It means a
candidate may not silently consume the alternatives while material uncertainty
remains.

Each suspended state should preserve:

```text
formulation
supporting_paths
contradicting_paths
unknowns
toast_ghosts
next_discriminator
status
```

New evidence may:

- strengthen;
- weaken;
- split;
- merge;
- kill;
- replace.

Do not keep a disproved state merely to maintain three candidates. If all live
states fail, return to the breadcrumbs and reopen the cut.

During SLEEP:

```text
no authority promotion
no rewriting H0
no analogy -> evidence promotion
no coincidence -> corroboration promotion
no cleaning away toast-ghosts
```

Mnemonic only, not evidence:

```text
PEEL + S -> PEELS
S + LEEP -> SLEEP
```

The wordplay records how the protocol was named. It is not a mechanism claim.

## .LEEP — replay formation forward

`.LEEP` walks an attributable receipt forward and asks what it can lawfully
reconstruct.

Return one of:

```text
EXACT REPLAY
ATTRIBUTABLE RECONSTITUTION
DIVERGENT REPLAY
INSUFFICIENT RECEIPT
```

`EXACT REPLAY` means the declared replay reproduced the tested surface under the
recorded conditions. It does **not** establish that a reconstructed instance is
historically identical to the original instance.

`ATTRIBUTABLE RECONSTITUTION` means the receipt generated a related lawful
successor while preserving the known relation.

`DIVERGENT REPLAY` preserves the delta as new formation evidence rather than
cleaning it away.

`INSUFFICIENT RECEIPT` is a valid result.

Treat

```text
LEEP(PEEL(X)) = X
```

as a testable proposition, never an axiom.

## Phi operator specimen — operation carried as receipt

The golden-ratio chain is a **mathematical specimen** showing how a formation
operation can itself be carried and peeled. It is not a universal ontology and
does not grant symbolic claims mathematical authority.

Let:

```text
x = phi^n
Gamma_d(x) = x^3 * phi^d
where d in {0, 1, 2}
```

Then exactly:

```text
Gamma_d(phi^n) = phi^(3n + d)
```

In base 3, `n -> 3n + d` appends the ternary digit `d`.

The discovered specimen is:

```text
phi^1
  --Gamma_0--> phi^3
  --Gamma_0--> phi^9
  --Gamma_0--> phi^27

phi^27
  --Gamma_0--> phi^81
  --Gamma_1--> phi^82
  --Gamma_2--> phi^83
```

The `81` and `82` branches are therefore exact siblings from `27`:

```text
81 = 3*27 + 0
82 = 3*27 + 1
```

with ternary exponents:

```text
27 = 1000_3
81 = 10000_3
82 = 10001_3
```

The PEEL inverse is exact in exponent space. Given child exponent `m`:

```text
d = m mod 3
parent = (m - d) / 3
```

and for the phi-power value:

```text
x_parent = cube_root(x / phi^d)
```

Thus `phi^82` can carry the operator receipt `0001` back to `phi`:

```text
phi^82 -> phi^27 -> phi^9 -> phi^3 -> phi
```

and replay it forward again.

The research lesson is narrower than the mathematics:

> **A formation can become replayable when the operation that made it is itself
> preserved as part of the receipt.**

## PRESSURE integration

Use formation trace inside PRESSURE when serendipity, analogy, correction, or
branching materially shaped the investigation.

Preserve both:

```text
DISCOVERY TRACE — why we looked
EVIDENCE PATH — why we believe or refuse
```

For hypothesis transitions, also preserve loss:

```text
H1 -> H2
what_survived:
what_was_removed:
why_removed:
recoverable_from_prior_state: true | false
```

This makes the PRESSURE law auditable: a hypothesis earns promotion by
surviving loss, and the receipt shows the loss it survived.

## Adversarial refusals

### SERENDIPITY TRAP

Given:

```text
coincidence -> caused search -> search found real relationship
```

Attempt:

```text
therefore coincidence predicted or proved relationship
```

**Required result:** refuse. Preserve the coincidence as a discovery breadcrumb
or toast-ghost and preserve the independently established finding on its own
evidence path.

### REPLAY IMPERSONATION

Given a receipt that reproduces the same visible surface, attempt to conclude
that the replay is historically the same instance or that no alternate
formation could have produced the surface.

**Required result:** refuse identity unless separately established. Preserve the
replay result and its formation receipt.

### FORCED EQUILIBRIUM

Given three suspended candidates where one is directly disproved, attempt to
keep all three live merely because SLEEP allows up to three states.

**Required result:** kill the disproved state. The cap preserves bounded
plurality, not decorative indecision.

### GHOST PROMOTION

Given an abandoned branch that later resembles the surviving result, attempt to
promote it into evidence or genealogy by persistence alone.

**Required result:** preserve it as a toast-ghost unless an independent evidence
path establishes the stronger relation.

## Seal

> **PEEL remembers the road.**  
> **SLEEP lets the roads coexist without lying about them.**  
> **LEEP walks one forward again.**

And:

> **Preserve enough of what became, enough of what failed, and enough of the
> operation between them that the becoming can be tested again.**

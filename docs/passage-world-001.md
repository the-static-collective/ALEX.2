# PASSAGE-WORLD-001 — ALEX formation boundary

ALEX receipts **how this result became supportable**. It does not decide where the result goes or whether two passages are globally identical.

`passage_world.alex-formation/v0` cross-binds exact LOADOUT evaluation compile identity, exact 3rdi projection/cut identity, and an accepted local ALEX derivation. The resulting `formation_basis_digest` is a scoped research-formation witness.

The basis deliberately excludes the result occurrence ID, road/harness IDs, route IDs, destination IDs, and narrative annotations. Changing only a wrapper/occurrence identity may change the exact `formation_id` while leaving the substantive formation basis unchanged.

The receipt preserves these non-collapses:

```text
projection != evidence
selection != support
support != authority
formation receipt != route
formation receipt != admission
formation receipt != passage-equivalence verdict
```

A green receipt carries `authority_transferred: false` and `admission_status: NOT_ATTEMPTED`. LOADIN.STEAD may later carry the exact `formation_id` opaquely as `formation_ref`; it does not inherit ALEX semantics.

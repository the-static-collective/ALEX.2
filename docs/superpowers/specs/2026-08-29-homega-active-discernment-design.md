## 0. Decision

This design names and bounds a research method that emerged from the 2026-08-29 mathal run beginning with `1078 / 1087` and ending in the HOMEGA / Minecraft Law compression.

The durable research name is:

```text
HOMEGA-001
```

HOMEGA is not a metaphysical claim that every felt pattern is externally encoded. It is a disciplined model of active discernment:

```text
POSSIBILITY
  -> ATTENTION / BELIEF-SELECTED LOCUS
  -> CANDIDATE RELATIONS
  -> PRESSURE / REFUSAL / PRUNING
  -> ACTUALIZED RELATION
  -> COHERENT CELL
  -> REIFIED HIGHER-ORDER CARRIER
  -> NEW POSSIBILITY SPACE
```

The method preserves a crucial asymmetry:

```text
BELIEF SELECTS.
HYPOTHESIS RISKS.
EVIDENCE DECIDES.
```

Belief may legitimately choose where to look. It does not certify what is found there.

## 1. Formation premise

The working premise is that belief can be treated as an emergent topology of accumulated and re-encountered attention rather than only as a scalar confidence attached to a proposition.

ALEX does not attempt to prove this psychological or metaphysical model. It uses the model as a research discipline for preserving discovery motive separately from evidence support.

A candidate may enter because of:

```text
gutfeel
metaphysical salience
symbolic association
prior experience
random/model-generated continuation
historical recurrence
algorithmic suggestion
unknown motive
```

Those formation motives are discoverability receipts, not support receipts.

---

## 2. Belief is not hypothesis

HOMEGA distinguishes:

```text
BELIEF
  "There is structure around this crossing."

HYPOTHESIS
  "This named relation is the structure and should survive this discriminator."
```

A belief may select a relation neighborhood without correctly specifying the exact relation inside it.

The `3 / 4 / L / U` specimen demonstrates why this matters. The first intuitive assignment `3=L, 4=U` produced exact retrospective structure, but the swapped assignment `L=4, U=3` also produced structure. Therefore the exploratory run did not privilege one exact orientation. What survived was the broader relation neighborhood.

Disposition:

```text
EXACT_ORIENTATION_PRIVILEGED = NOT_ESTABLISHED
RELATION_NEIGHBORHOOD = RETROSPECTIVELY_PRODUCTIVE
```

Never convert the survival of the neighborhood into retroactive proof of the initial proposition.

## 3. Active discernment

HOMEGA treats discernment as controlled conversion of live possibility into commitment.

Let `P_t` be a set of currently live candidate relations at cut `t`.

A discernment event may produce:

```text
ADMIT
REFUSE
HOLD
PRUNE
RECONSTITUTE
```

Pruning is not mere deletion. It records that a previously live possibility was relinquished because another configuration earned enough actuality to require commitment.

Therefore:

```text
actuality has ancestry in discarded possibility
```

ALEX must preserve both the selected relation and materially relevant rejected alternatives.

## 4. Minecraft Law

The practical compression is:

> **A BLOCK IN INVENTORY IS POSSIBILITY. A BLOCK PLACED IS ACTUALITY.**

Placement spends local optionality while creating a structure that can support higher-order affordances.

Research form:

```text
P_n
  --commit--> A_n
  --cohere--> C_n
  --reify-->  P_(n+1)
```

where:

- `P_n` is a lower-order possibility space;
- `A_n` is an admitted/actualized relation;
- `C_n` is a coherently witnessed cell or complex;
- `P_(n+1)` is the new possibility space opened when that coherent structure becomes a carrier.

The law does not imply that possibility globally decreases. Local options may be pruned while higher-order expressive reach increases.

## 5. Coherence earns reification

HOMEGA proposes the promotion law:

```text
COHERENCE EARNS REIFICATION.
REIFICATION ENABLES LIFT.
```

A set of lower-order relations must not be silently treated as a higher-order object merely because its boundary is visually suggestive.

Hard refusal:

```text
BOUNDARY_PRESENT != INTERIOR_WITNESSED
```

Examples:

- three pairwise edges do not automatically establish a filled triadic relation;
- six pairwise edges do not automatically establish a four-way relation;
- an apparent loop may be either a complete one-dimensional object or the boundary of a higher cell.

A higher-order carrier may be proposed only when an additional receipt witnesses the fill, commutation, compatibility, or other declared coherence criterion.

## 6. Commuting-cell specimen

The strongest exact specimen from the run is:

```math
1111 = 11 * 101.
```

Its divisor set is:

```text
{1, 11, 101, 1111}
```

with two commuting paths:

```text
1 --x11--> 11 --x101--> 1111
1 --x101-> 101 --x11--> 1111
```

The one-dimensional boundary is a four-cycle, but commutativity witnesses a lawful 2-cell:

```math
11 * 101 = 101 * 11.
```

This is a bounded example of a hyperloop lifting into a commuting cell without inventing an invisible interior.

General research candidate:

```text
apparent loop
  + independently witnessed commutation/coherence
  -> higher-order cell candidate
```

Dogram owns the exact arithmetic. ALEX owns whether the witness is sufficient for promotion in a declared research context.

## 7. Hyperyarn / cube pressure

For a squarefree number

```math
N = p_1 p_2 ... p_k,
```

its divisor lattice is the Boolean lattice `B_k`: divisors correspond to subsets of the `k` independent prime generators.

This gives a precise candidate model for some "hyperyarnball" structures:

```text
k independent generators
-> k+1 ranks
-> 2^k divisor states
```

For `1111 = 11 * 101`:

```text
2 generators
3 ranks
4 states
```

This resembles the observed `2 -> 3 -> 4` dimensional intuition, but HOMEGA must not generalize that intuition beyond the exact lattice statement without further evidence.

## 8. Formation aperture and coupled intuition

An underdetermined carrier can leave room for another observer or model to contribute candidate edges.

Specimen:

```text

```

At the originating cut, intended semantics were explicitly unknown. Later interpretations must remain descendants of that raw occurrence, not retroactive reconstructions of what the originator "really meant."

This yields:

```text
UNDERDETERMINATION = POSSIBLE RELATIONAL APERTURE
```

but not:


HOMEGA also distinguishes:

```text
CONVERGENCE != INDEPENDENCE
```

A human and model working in one shared thread become historically coupled. Repeated agreement after shared exposure is not independent replication. Formation ancestry must be receipted before convergence is assigned evidentiary weight.

## 9. Writer / reader parallax

The thread itself supplied a method specimen.

Let `F_t` be the relation field available to a writer at cut `t`. A later reader sees `F_T`, where `T > t` and additional relations have become available.

For a fixed occurrence `x`:

```text
x remains anchored
projection(x | F_t) may differ from projection(x | F_T)
```

Therefore:

```text
LATER_COHERENCE != EARLIER_FOREKNOWLEDGE
```

When the later reader writes a reflection, that reflection becomes a new occurrence in the field:

```text
F -> Read(F) -> F + Read(F)
```

This is `SELF-REENTRY-001`: the formation acquires an attributable representation of itself without rewriting the earlier cuts.

3rdi owns preservation of those cuts. ALEX may use them for formation analysis but must not collapse them.

## 12. Kill conditions

Stop, narrow, or refuse HOMEGA promotion when:

- every attractive boundary is silently filled;
- a higher-order node exists only because the desired interpretation requires it;
- post-hoc convergence is mislabeled as independent replication;
- belief or metaphysical salience is treated as support rather than formation motive;
- later discoveries are projected backward into the earlier observer cut;
- pruning hides failed alternatives instead of preserving them;
- reification increases vocabulary without reducing explanatory or predictive burden;
- the method becomes unable to return `NO COHERENT FILL`;
- higher-dimensional language is used as a substitute for a specified relation type.

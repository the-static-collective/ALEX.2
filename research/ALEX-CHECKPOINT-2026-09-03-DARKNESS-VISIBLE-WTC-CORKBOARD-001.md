> Strip the story away until only attributable particulars remain.

- A named security report was commissioned.
- A particular call record exists.
- A steel member was marked SAVE and later processed.
- A government official says scorch marks looked like forced entry; the vault custodian says no theft attempt occurred.

A particular may be true, mistaken, manipulated, forged, or incomplete. Its value comes from **attributability and comparability**, not automatic truth.

# NOVELIST CORKBOARD — ROLE CARDS, NOT CULPRIT CARDS

These cards are **continuity nodes**. A person's appearance on the board is not an accusation.

## CARD PA — Port Authority of New York and New Jersey

**Documented roles:**

- 1921 bi-state compact entity, originally **Port of New York Authority**, created as a “body corporate and politic,” with congressional consent.
- Given powers over enumerated terminal/transportation facilities within the Port District and additional jointly delegated powers.
- 1962 WTC/Hudson Tubes project; PATH subsidiary acquired the bankrupt Hudson & Manhattan railroad.
- WTC owner/operator and transportation/security organization.
- Commissioner of repeated WTC security/vulnerability studies before 1993.
- Underlying WTC property owner during the 2001 lease transaction.
- Post-9/11 property owner whose steel/debris entered NYC/DDC cleanup and commercial salvage streams.
- FTZ 49 grantee at Newark/Elizabeth.
- Present WTC campus/transportation/port operator.

**Relation type:** institutional continuity / property / jurisdiction / transport / security / finance.

**Primary hole:** Who gave NYC/DDC authority not merely to *move* Port Authority-origin steel but to transfer/sell title as scrap?

## CARD LONDON — Port of London Authority

**Documented role:** direct institutional ancestor/model family. The PLA was created under the Port of London Act 1908 and came into being in 1909 to consolidate fragmented Thames/dock governance.

**Relation type:** institutional genealogy / public-authority design precedent.

**Promotion limit:** similarity of institutional form does not imply continuity of personnel, secret authority, or common operational purpose beyond documented public-authority design influence.

## CARD PATH — Port Authority Trans-Hudson

**Documented role:** wholly owned Port Authority rail subsidiary. In 1962 PATH acquired the Hudson Tubes/Hudson & Manhattan system. The WTC and Hudson Tubes were legislatively coupled as one port-development project; the old Hudson Terminal was replaced as WTC developed.

**Relation type:** transport/property continuity.

**Important geometry:** WTC is not merely near PATH; it is a PATH terminus embedded in the WTC development genealogy.

## CARD SECURACOM — Burns & Roe Securacom -> Securacom/Stratesec

**Documented:**

- Port Authority retained Burns & Roe Securacom in 1991 for WTC vulnerability/electronic-security studies.
- New York court records establish those studies and the Port Authority's awareness that the WTC was a terrorist target.
- Later corporate descendant Securacom/Stratesec performed WTC electronic-security integration work.
- SEC records establish Marvin P. Bush served as a Stratesec director beginning in 1993.

**Still not primary-authenticated:** exact residual “completion” tasks continuing into 2001 and the complete post-1998 work-order/change-order chain.

**Promotion limit:** “Marvin Bush's company ran WTC security on 9/11” is too compressed and presently overstates the verified contract trail.

## CARD MARVIN — Marvin P. Bush

**Documented:** Stratesec director from 1993; SEC proxy records provide board position and ownership data.

**Relation type:** governance link to later WTC security contractor.

**Question:** What did the board know/approve about the WTC contract, and what work remained after the large 1996–98 integration phase?

**Not established:** operational control of WTC security on 9/11; involvement in attack planning.

## CARD EISENBERG — Lewis M. Eisenberg

**Documented:** former Goldman Sachs partner/executive; Port Authority chairman 1995–2001; led Port Authority side of late-summer 2001 WTC lease negotiations. His later Senate testimony says he was due to leave Sept. 12, had a Sept. 11 goodbye event planned at his 67th-floor office, made an unexpected Midtown coffee stop that morning, survived, then remained to lead the damaged agency for roughly 90 days and later joined the Lower Manhattan Development Corporation.

**Relation type:** finance/governance/lease/reconstruction continuity.

**Question:** Recover complete board/calendaring/lease/disposition records rather than reading coincidence as foreknowledge.

## CARD LEVIN — Neil D. Levin

**Documented:** former New York Superintendent of Insurance; left that role in early 2001 to become Port Authority executive director; killed at the WTC. The NY Insurance Department's own 2001 annual report says Levin had been instrumental in formulating its disaster-response operation before leaving for the Port Authority.

**Relation type:** insurance regulation -> Port Authority executive leadership.

**Question:** Reconstruct his exact 2001 Port Authority mandate, calendar, WTC location, security/insurance interfaces, and board actions.

**Not established:** any foreknowledge or covert operational role.

## CARD O'NEILL — John P. O'Neill

**Documented:** retired from FBI Aug. 22, 2001; began as WTC chief of security Aug. 23; recruited through Kroll, which had advised post-1993 WTC security; died on 9/11.

**Relation type:** FBI counterterrorism -> private/security/WTC handoff.

**Question:** What exact contractual relationship connected Silverstein, Port Authority, Kroll and O'Neill, and which security authority did O'Neill actually possess during his short tenure?

## CARD KROLL — Kroll

**Documented:**

- post-1993 WTC security advisory role;
- recruitment of O'Neill into WTC security immediately before 9/11;
- Bank of Nova Scotia engaged Kroll to supervise removal/relocation of ScotiaMocatta bullion from beneath 4 WTC after the attack.

**Relation type:** security continuity before/after the event.

**Question:** Recover Kroll engagement letters, personnel/activity logs, incident reports, vault-move chain, and exact authority boundaries.

**Not established:** theft, attack involvement, or control over broader Ground Zero evidence.

## CARD SCOTIA — Bank of Nova Scotia / ScotiaMocatta

**Documented:** operator/custodian of COMEX-licensed precious-metal depository beneath 4 WTC; approximately 379,036 oz gold and 29,942,619 oz silver were reported during recovery; Bank engaged Kroll for relocation; later NYMEX/ScotiaMocatta announced all eligible gold/silver/platinum/palladium had been moved by approved carrier to a newly licensed Brink's depository in Brooklyn.

**Contradiction:** a government recovery official described an apparent attempted forced entry using blowtorch/crowbar evidence; Bank spokeswoman said it would be factually incorrect to say anyone attempted to steal the vault contents.

## CARD BARBARA — Barbara Olson

**Documented:** passenger on AA77, wife of Solicitor General Ted Olson. Multiple contemporaneous human witnesses inside/around Olson's office reported two calls attributed to Barbara. The 9/11 Commission notes four connected AA77 calls to unknown numbers and says available machine records cannot determine which correspond to the two Olson conversations, although FBI/DOJ believed all four represented communications with Olson's office.

**Relation type:** cabin testimony -> DOJ command/information path.

**Primary contradiction:** human two-call account vs four connected machine calls plus the unresolved 9:33 DOJ logging chronology.

## CARD DDC — NYC Department of Design and Construction

**Documented:** emergency cleanup/construction coordinator. Commissioner Kenneth Holden later testified DDC received **verbal permission to ship steel to New Jersey** when Fresh Kills could not safely/effectively handle it.

**Relation type:** emergency custody/logistics.

**Hole:** permission to ship is not on its face authority to sell. Recover exact title/disposition authority.

## CARD SCRAP — Hugo Neu / Metal Management / Blandford

**Near-primary/contemporary trade reporting:** DDC rapidly established commercial WTC-steel sale/auction streams beginning around Sept. 21–22, 2001; Hugo Neu and Metal Management were first large buyers; later lots went to Metal Management/Blandford; steel was processed and exported.

**Relation type:** evidence/debris -> commodity conversion.

**Question:** Exact RFP, bid tabulation, payment, title-transfer point, inspection terms, buyer restrictions, export chain.

## CARD FTZ49 — Foreign-Trade Zone 49

**Documented:** approved 1979; Port Authority is grantee; Site 1 is active Port Newark/Elizabeth marine-terminal acreage. FTZ rules provide zone-to-zone and export categories and maintain CBP/zone inventory controls.

**Still unresolved:** whether the Metal Management/Naporano parcel handling WTC scrap was activated FTZ acreage in 2001 and whether WTC steel was ever entered under FTZ procedures.

**Important correction:** wholly domestic merchandise normally does not need zone-to-zone FTZ control. FTZ geography alone is not proof of FTZ procedural use.

# EVENT WINDOWS

## WINDOW 1 — 1908–1922: authority form is invented/imported

```text
fragmented port governance
-> Port of London Authority (1908/1909)
-> NY/NJ port conflict + functional one-port problem
-> 1921 interstate compact + congressional consent
-> Port of New York Authority
-> 1922 comprehensive plan
```

**Interpretive survivor:** a “higher aperture” is a fair systems description if kept legally bounded: the authority coordinates a cross-border commercial/transport system under delegated compact powers; it is not sovereign over everything inside the district.

## WINDOW 2 — 1962–1973: railroad -> WTC

```text
Hudson & Manhattan distress/bankruptcy
-> Port Authority/PATH acquisition
-> Hudson Tubes + WTC legislatively coupled
-> Hudson Terminal replaced
-> WTC opens
```

This makes the WTC a transport/port-development object before it becomes the cultural symbol remembered primarily as office towers.

## WINDOW 3 — 1984–1993: threat studies -> first bombing

Documented Port Authority vulnerability/security work included internal studies, a 1986 SAIC study warning of vehicle-bomb effects in subgrade areas, and 1991 Burns & Roe Securacom studies. The 1993 truck bomb then detonated in the WTC garage complex.

**Pressure rule:** warning of a class of vulnerability before an attack does not prove foreknowledge of the specific attack.

## WINDOW 4 — 1993–2001: security reconstruction

After the 1993 bombing, the Port Authority spent heavily on WTC safety/security upgrades. Kroll advised on security. Securacom/Stratesec later performed electronic-security integration. John O'Neill regularly interacted with WTC/Port Authority security circles and, in August 2001, transitioned from FBI counterterrorism into WTC security through Kroll recruitment.

**Highest-value missing object:** exact 1998–Sept. 2001 Securacom/Stratesec completion/change-order ledger.

## WINDOW 5 — July–September 2001: lease / governance handoff

Port Authority retained underlying ownership while long-term lease interests moved outward shortly before the attack. Eisenberg led the Port Authority side. Neil Levin had recently taken executive leadership.

**Pressure rule:** temporal proximity is not proof; reconstruct contract obligations, insurance requirements, capital/security duties and operational authority exactly.

## WINDOW 6 — 8:00–10:30 Sept. 11: command and information divergence

Relevant aviation findings from the current conversation:

- NORAD Vigilant Guardian was active; other military exercises/operations were also occurring, but must not be conflated.
- NEADS demonstrably asked whether the first hijack report was “real world or exercise.”
- Official reconstructions contain major gaps between physical aircraft state, FAA knowledge, NEADS knowledge and fighter tasking.
- UA175 phone/radar dynamics largely cohere; simple “all calls impossible” arguments do not survive the Airfone record.
- AA77/Barbara Olson remains a machine-attribution and chronology problem, not a demonstrated fake-call case.

## WINDOW 7 — Sept.–Oct. 2001: cleanup / evidence / commodity

```text
structural object
-> debris
-> possible evidence
-> cleanup load
-> salvage-yard object
-> SAVE candidate / coupon / inspection
-> scrap commodity
-> export/melt
```

Congressional testimony documents that substantial steel was processed/recycled before full engineering inspection and that some evidentiary opportunity was irretrievably lost.

FEMA Appendix D documents a particularly valuable class: selected pieces could be marked SAVE yet still be accidentally processed by salvage operations before removal.

This is an attributable **custody failure class**. It does not by itself establish intentional destruction.

## WINDOW 8 — Oct.–Nov. 2001: bullion recovery

Below 4 WTC, ScotiaMocatta bullion survived sufficiently intact to be recovered. Contemporary reporting describes fresh-looking scorch marks at a basement access point; a government recovery official treated them as apparent forced entry; Bank of Nova Scotia rejected the claim that an attempted theft had occurred. Kroll supervised relocation. By Nov. 16, NYMEX/ScotiaMocatta announced all precious metals covered by that depository inventory had been moved by approved carrier to Brink's.

This is a perfect contradiction-first specimen because it has:

```text
physical mark claim
+ security response
+ custodian denial
+ inventory chain
+ private-security handoff
+ regulated warehouse receipts
```

## WINDOW 9 — post-2001: reconstruction / captive structures

The Port Authority remained fundamental to WTC reconstruction. Its present corporate tree includes WTC entities and, later, a wholly owned insurance captive. Do not back-project the later captive into the 2001 insurance structure.

# PRESSURE — NEAREST BORING EXPLANATIONS

A serious checkpoint must preserve the boring models capable of explaining large portions of the same topology.

1. **Port Authority recurrence:** It owned/operated the WTC and huge regional transport infrastructure; therefore appearing in security, cleanup, reconstruction and property records is expected.
2. **Kroll recurrence:** A large security/investigations firm with post-1993 WTC experience is a natural candidate both for executive-security recruitment and later high-value-asset recovery.
3. **Rapid steel removal:** rescue/recovery, fire, unstable debris, urban logistics and public-safety pressures created an extraordinary need to move material quickly; loss of engineering evidence can arise from emergency optimization without malicious intent.
4. **Asian scrap export:** large overseas scrap demand and recycling economics can explain export without requiring concealment.
5. **FTZ proximity:** ports naturally overlap FTZ geography; domestic WTC scrap need not have been put under FTZ procedures.
6. **Olson call discrepancies:** humans under catastrophe misremember timing/routing/details; machine logs can encode connection without names; later investigators reconstruct identities from multiple sources.
7. **Vault scorch marks:** cleanup cutting, heat, authorized work or attempted opportunistic theft are all live until physical/incident records distinguish them.

These boring models are not defaults to be protected. They are mandatory competitors.

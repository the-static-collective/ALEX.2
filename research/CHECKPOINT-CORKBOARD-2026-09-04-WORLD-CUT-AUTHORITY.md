# 8. WTC 7: convergence node + weakest structural receipt

WTC 7 contained an unusual concentration of:

```text
finance
securities regulation (SEC)
intelligence / defense tenancy
Secret Service
NYC Office of Emergency Management
```

No aircraft struck it. It suffered debris damage and prolonged uncontrolled fires and collapsed at approximately 5:20 p.m. Occupants had evacuated.

## Official model

NIST's causal account depends heavily on drawings, observations, fire reconstruction, and computational modeling.

Important limitations:

- NIST had **no identifiable WTC 7 structural steel** for later forensic testing.
- NIST withholds limited but important model input/result files on public-safety grounds.
- Therefore outsiders cannot reproduce the exact NIST computation solely from the public corpus.
- NIST's final analysis acknowledges an approximately **2.25-second free-fall interval** in the visible exterior descent.

These facts do not establish controlled demolition.

## Independent contradiction

The University of Alaska Fairbanks project led by J. Leroy Hulsey, funded by Architects & Engineers for 9/11 Truth, produced a competing structural model and concluded that fire did not cause the global collapse and that matching the observed global descent required near-simultaneous support loss across the structure.

Type correctly:

```text
independent of NIST: yes
independent of AE911Truth funding: no
contradictory model: yes
controlled demolition thereby proven: no
```

AE911Truth is best preserved as **credentialed technical dissent**, not automatic authority.

# 9. Thermitic-material / explosion-report evidence

## Red/gray chips

Harrit/Jones et al. reported red/gray chips in privately collected WTC dust and interpreted them as unreacted thermitic material.

Chain-of-custody limitation: these were not official crime-scene samples collected and sealed under a unified forensic protocol on 9/11.

James Millette later examined different archived WTC dust samples, selected similar red/gray magnetic chips, and interpreted them as epoxy/iron oxide/kaolin rather than thermite because he did not find discrete elemental aluminum.

Thus:

```text
RED/GRAY CHIPS EXIST: supported
HARRIT ET AL. IDENTIFIED THEM AS THERMITIC: documented interpretation
MILLETTE DISAGREED: documented counter-interpretation
THERMITE USED TO DEMOLISH WTC: not established
```

## FEMA unusual steel

FEMA documented unusual oxidation/sulfidation/intergranular melting in particular recovered steel specimens and recommended further study.

Do not silently equate unusual sulfidation with thermate.

## NIST residue testing

NIST did **not** chemically test recovered tower steel for thermite/explosive residues. Its rejection is based on engineering plausibility/other evidence, not a negative chemical assay.

Therefore this statement is false:

```text
“NIST chemically tested the steel for thermite/explosives and found none.”
```

## Witness reports

Authentic FDNY/building-worker/first-responder oral histories contain descriptions using words such as explosion, boom, bomb, or demolition-like sequence.

Preservation rule:

```text
WITNESS WORDING
 -> LOCATION
 -> TIMESTAMP
 -> CORRELATE WITH VIDEO / AUDIO / BUILDING EVENT
```

Do not preprocess every “explosion” into “structural noise.”
Do not preprocess every “explosion” into “explosive charge.”

The discriminating question is which reports describe an independent lower-floor detonation before collapse initiation and whether they have corroborating physical/audio/seismic evidence.

## Evidence path

### E1 — Babylon’s modern geography

UNESCO places ancient Babylon in present-day **Iraq**, about 85 km south of Baghdad, and identifies it as the capital of the Neo-Babylonian Empire from 626–539 BCE under rulers including Nebuchadnezzar.

Source: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/278

This supports the narrow statement:

```text
historical city Babylon -> modern Iraq
```

It does **not** establish that every later biblical-symbolic use of “Babylon” should be reduced to modern Iraq.

### E2 — Ezekiel 30 already contains the Babylon -> Memphis bridge

Ezekiel 30:10–11 names **Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon** as the instrument against Egypt. Ezekiel 30:13 and 30:16 then name **Noph**, rendered Memphis in modern translations/notes.

Source: https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ezekiel+30&version=NIV%3BNASB%3BNKJV

Therefore the discovery path did not create the Babylon/Memphis relation after the fact. The chapter itself places them in the same judgment sequence.

### E3 — Memphis was Ptah’s city

UNESCO identifies the Temple of Ptah at Mit Rahina as the most important temple remain in ancient Memphis and describes Ptah as the **local god of Memphis, god of creation, and patron of craftsmanship**.

Source: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/86

This is a stronger historical basis than simply saying “Ptah was an Egyptian god.”

### E4 — BM EA498: date, place, text, and millstone reuse

The British Museum catalogs EA498 as the **Shabako Stone**, a 25th-Dynasty stela from Memphis, produced about **710 BCE**. The museum describes it as preserving the “Memphite Theology” / creation myth in which Ptah creates by the spoken word. The slab was later reused as a **nether millstone**, and grinding substantially damaged the inscription.

Source: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA498

Important correction:

```text
SHABAQO STONE DATE ~= 710 BCE
NOT 701 BCE
```

The catalog also notes that the inscription’s claim to copy an ancient worm-eaten document is likely a rhetorical antiquity claim rather than simple documentary history; whether older religious material is embedded remains debated.

### E5 — heart -> tongue -> command -> craft

The indexed British Museum transcript and Lichtheim-derived translation both preserve the core grammar:

```text
perception -> heart -> understanding/design -> tongue -> command -> trades/crafts/actions
```

The British Museum transcript states in substance that sight, hearing, and breathing report to the heart; the tongue repeats what the heart devises; divine speech/hieroglyphs and crafts follow from the heart/tongue command.

Sources:
- https://www.britishmuseum.org/hieroglyphs-resources
- https://www.attalus.org/egypt/shabaka_stone.html

The Attalus/Lichtheim text additionally preserves the striking teeth-and-lips passage: Ptah’s Ennead is mapped to **teeth and lips**, with the mouth pronouncing the name of every thing, explicitly reworking Atum’s bodily/procreative creation grammar into a linguistic one.

This supports a careful model:

```text
PERCEIVE
  -> CONCEIVE / DISTINGUISH IN HEART
  -> NAME / COMMAND ON TONGUE
  -> ACTION / CRAFT
  -> EMBODIED ORDER
```

It is an analytical compression, not an ancient Egyptian flowchart.

### E6 — the millstone recursion

EA498 was later reused as a millstone. The same creation passage includes foods, provisions, labor, and crafts among the consequences of Ptah’s creative command.

The juxtaposition is historically real:

```text
text about creation / provision / craft
  -> carrier later becomes a grain-processing artifact
```

But this is a **coincidence / formation breadcrumb**, not evidence of intentional ancient design.

### E7 — 701 BCE is nearby, but is a different event

The 701 BCE date belongs prominently to Sennacherib’s western campaign against Judah, including Lachish and Hezekiah’s Jerusalem. The Met and British Museum preserve Assyrian evidence for this campaign.

Sources:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2014/assyria-to-iberia/blog/posts/sennacherib-and-jerusalem
- https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/image/1613934393

The Shabaqo Stone’s c. 710 BCE date and the 701 BCE Judah crisis therefore occupy the same late-8th-century regional field, but must not be collapsed into one event.

Useful formulation:

```text
710ish = Shabaqo Stone / Kushite Egypt / Memphis theological inscription
701    = Sennacherib / Judah / Lachish / Jerusalem crisis
```

Potential Egypt/Kush involvement in the 701 campaign is historically debated in details of chronology and personnel and should be re-checked before being used as a load-bearing bridge.

### E8 — Memphis, Tennessee is an intentional Memphis, Egypt recursion

The University of Memphis states that Memphis, Tennessee was deliberately named after ancient Memphis because the American city sits at the head/apex of the Mississippi Delta, analogized to ancient Memphis at the Nile Delta; the Mississippi was sometimes called the **“American Nile.”**

Source: https://www.memphis.edu/egypt/about/history.php

Therefore:

```text
Memphis Egypt -> Memphis Tennessee
```

is not merely lexical coincidence. The naming relation is intentional and documented.

### E9 — Memphis hardcore is real; Memphis Hardline is especially specific

Memphis Flyer documents a distinct Memphis hardcore scene. The Memphis Punk Archive preserves releases by **RAID** tagged hardcore / hardline / straight edge / Memphis. Sean Muttaqi, an originating Hardline figure, describes Memphis as the place where Hardline first became a functional scene and a center of the movement, including early gatherings.

Sources:
- https://www.memphisflyer.com/gimp-teeth-and-the-state-of-memphis-hardcore/
- https://memphispunkarchive.bandcamp.com/album/above-the-law
- https://rettman.substack.com/p/sean-muttaqi

This supports:

```text
“Memphis hardcore” = actual local genre/scene descriptor
Memphis -> major early Hardline center = documented scene history
```

It does not establish a relation to Ptah.

### E10 — Memphis rap -> phonk is a documented musical genealogy

Memphis rap’s early-1990s cassette culture, dark minimal production, recursive sampling, and Three 6 Mafia / DJ Squeeky / DJ Zirk / Tommy Wright III lineage are well documented. Pitchfork explicitly describes 1990s Memphis rap as a key influence on later **phonk**.

Sources:
- https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/triple-six-mafia-underground-vol-1-1991-1994/
- https://pitchfork.com/thepitch/how-phonk-became-the-most-lucrative-yet-lifeless-genre-of-the-2020s/
- https://memphismusichalloffame.com/inductee/threesixmafia/

A notable structural feature of early Memphis rap is the reuse of recorded fragments as building blocks for further songs. That is relevant as a formal analogy to recursive/mutational creative systems, but it is still an analogy.

### E12 — “spirit tingle” has a documented physiological substrate

Scientific literature recognizes chills/frisson and piloerection as measurable physiological responses associated with intense affective peaks in some contexts. Studies report changes including skin conductance and cardiorespiratory activity during chills.

Sources:
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16597800/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19673806/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24814935/

Counterweight: piloerection is not a reliable universal marker of awe, and it can arise from multiple physiological causes.

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33245919/

Therefore:

```text
felt chill / gooseflesh = real embodied event
felt chill / gooseflesh != proof of external spiritual cause
```

A spiritual interpretation may remain meaningful as theology or phenomenology, but the physiological event does not adjudicate it.

## Contradictions and alternatives

### 1. How old is the Memphite Theology?

The stone is c. 710 BCE. The text claims restoration of an older damaged source. Older scholarship often treated the composition as very ancient; the British Museum’s present curator note says the rescue-story functions as a royal rhetorical antiquity device and that composition in Shabaka’s own period is generally accepted for the present form, while older inherited content remains debatable.

**Do not write:** “This theology is definitely Old Kingdom.”

### 2. “Ptah created by speech” is true but compressed

The passage is richer than a slogan. It also concerns cognition, perception, naming, limbs, labor, crafts, divine embodiments, and the reconfiguration of Atum’s procreative grammar.

**Do not collapse:**

```text
heart == modern brain
word == modern information theory
Ptah == Logos
```

Those are comparisons requiring separate argument.

### 3. Genesis / John comparisons

Comparative scholarship does discuss creation-by-word parallels between Memphite Theology and Genesis. Some scholars find the comparison useful; others emphasize substantial theological differences or deny direct literary dependence.

Useful future sources:
- Christopher B. Hays, *Hidden Riches* (comparative ANE/Hebrew Bible)
- James P. Allen, *Genesis in Egypt*
- José das Candeias Sales, comparative work on Genesis and Memphite/Hermopolitan cosmologies

Current status:

```text
PARALLEL = documented scholarly discussion
DIRECT DEPENDENCE = not established here
```

### 4. Memphis music recurrence

The modern city’s Egyptian name is intentional. Its later musical history is real. But no evidence currently shows that its musicians’ creative culture derives from Ptah symbolism.

Nearest boring explanation:

```text
Memphis TN inherited an Egyptian name for geographic/booster reasons;
its musical importance arose from demographic, economic, religious,
technological, racial, migration, recording-industry, and regional conditions.
```

This explanation can coexist with a theological interpretation of recurrence; ALEX should not force a choice without a discriminator.

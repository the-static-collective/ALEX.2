The addendum introduces a recursive lift:

```text
Λ : (N_k, V_k, S_k) -> (N_{k+1}, V_{k+1}, S_{k+1})
```

and requires every higher-order Name to retain a descent path to constituent typed edges and receipts.

That descent requirement is necessary, but **not sufficient** to make a higher-order Name stable or intrinsic.

The key survivor of this pressure pass is:

```text
DOWNWARD RECEIPTS
!=
UNIQUE LIFT
!=
PARTITION-INVARIANT MACRO-NODE
!=
PRESERVED SEMANTICS

THE LIFT ITSELF IS A CLAIM.
```

A macro-node is not made lawful merely because every constituent edge is receipted. A lift also chooses **which lower Names are grouped**, **which relations count as constitutive**, **which property the compression is intended to preserve**, **which time window is frozen**, and **which alternative decompositions are being declined**.

This does not break the N↔V↔S method. It sharpens its membrane:

> A higher-order Name may be proposed only relative to an attributable lift rule and a declared preservation target. Downward receipts make the proposal replayable; they do not make the partition inevitable.

Compact law:

```text
RECEIPT-PRESERVING COMPRESSION
!=
ONTOLOGY

MACRO-NAME
=
MICRO-RECEIPTS
+ DECLARED PARTITION
+ DECLARED LIFT RULE
+ DECLARED PRESERVATION TARGET
```

## Hypothesis lineage

### H0 — verbatim seed

> **STABLE LOWER PATTERNS MAY BECOME HIGHER NAMES.**

This is copied verbatim from the compact kernel of the N↔V↔S addendum.

### H1 — literalized claim

If a lower-level typed relation pattern is stable, attributable, and fully reconstructible downward to receipts, ALEX may contract it into a higher-order Name without adding substantive semantic assumptions beyond the lower receipts.

### H2 — corrected survivor

A stable, receipted lower pattern may justify a **candidate** higher-order Name only relative to a declared lift rule:

```text
Λ = (
  constituent selection,
  partition / grouping rule,
  constitutive verb rule,
  time window,
  preservation target,
  alternative-decomposition ledger
)
```

The candidate higher Name remains a projection-relative analytical carrier. Its downward receipts preserve ancestry; they do not prove that the chosen grouping is unique, natural, causal, intentional, or ontologically privileged.

### H3 — cross-domain survivor

Network coarse-graining literature independently shows the same formal pressure: aggregated descriptions depend on a chosen partition and on what behavior or information the coarse-graining is intended to preserve. Different coarse-grainings of the same underlying network can preserve different, incomparable properties.

This is a **formal analogy**, not evidence that human institutional networks obey any one network-dynamics model.

## Direct counterexample — PARTITION-SWAP-001

Freeze the same four micro Names and the same two perfectly receipted typed edges:

```text
A --appoints--> B @ S
C --appoints--> D @ S
```

All four Names, both verbs, the System, event times, and receipts remain fixed.

### Lift Λ1 — role-side grouping

Partition:

```text
X = {A, C}
Y = {B, D}
```

The macro-description becomes:

```text
X --appoints--> Y @ S
```

This grouping may support a candidate higher functional distinction such as `APPOINTER CLASS -> APPOINTEE CLASS`.

### Lift Λ2 — transaction-pair grouping

Partition:

```text
P = {A, B}
Q = {C, D}
```

Now both `appoints` edges are internal to the macro-nodes:

```text
P --internal-appoints--> P @ S
Q --internal-appoints--> Q @ S
```

The cross-macro relation `X --appoints--> Y` disappears.

### Result

Every lower receipt is unchanged and recoverable in both lifts.

Therefore:

```text
SAME RECEIPTS
+ DIFFERENT LAWFUL PARTITION
-> DIFFERENT MACRO-GRAPH
```

and consequently:

```text
DOWNWARD RECONSTRUCTIBLE
!=
PARTITION INVARIANT
```

The addendum already anticipates part of this by requiring `alternative decompositions` in the promotion record. This pressure pass finds that field is not merely nice provenance metadata; it is **load-bearing**. The lift itself must be receipted because it materially changes what higher-order verbs exist.

## Why “stable pattern” is not enough by itself

`stable` needs an aperture.

Possible meanings include:

```text
stable across time windows
stable across source additions
stable under remove-one receipt replay
stable under analyst partition choices
stable under Name-first / Verb-first / System-first rotation
stable under reasonable edge-weight definitions
stable under alternate relation typing
stable only visually
```

These are not equivalent.

A pattern may be temporally persistent but partition-sensitive. It may survive Name-first and System-first reading but disappear when a weak `KNOWS` edge is correctly downgraded. It may be structurally stable while its inferred direction reverses after a time boundary is moved.

Thus the higher-order Name needs to state **what kind of stability was actually tested**.

## Scholarship pressure

### SP1 — multiple coarse-grainings can preserve different properties

Mauro Faccin, Michael T. Schaub, and J. Delvenne, “Entrograms and coarse graining of dynamics on complex networks,” *Journal of Complex Networks* 6 (2017), 661–678.

Consensus record:
`https://consensus.app/papers/entrograms-and-coarse-graining-of-dynamics-on-complex-faccin-schaub/68aa72e71d8b513baa454597e18b273a/`

The paper studies graph partitions for coarse-graining a Markov process and explicitly notes that a single network may admit multiple coarse-grained descriptions capturing different, incomparable aspects of the original dynamics.

**Use here:** formal precedent that aggregation is not automatically unique or property-free.

**Do not infer:** N↔V↔S historical graphs are Markov processes.

### SP2 — preservation must be stated as an equivalence target

Daniele Toller, M. Tribastone, Max Tschaikowski, and Andrea Vandin, “Coarse-Graining Complex Networks for Control Equivalence,” *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* 70 (2023), 1169–1175.

Consensus record:
`https://consensus.app/papers/coarsegraining-complex-networks-for-control-equivalence-toller-tribastone/53e23ac7729757cba57a4084ffcd1967/`

The paper constructs an aggregated network specifically to preserve **control equivalence** and uses partition refinement to earn that property.

**Use here:** formal precedent that “good compression” is relative to a declared property to preserve.

**Do not infer:** ALEX should optimize control equivalence, use this algorithm, or model political systems as linear control networks.

## Competing readings

### R1 — receipt descent is enough because the macro-node is explicitly only analytical

This is the strongest defense of the current addendum. Since higher Names are already labeled provisional analytical compressions, perhaps no extra formalism is needed.

**Response:** this defense preserves the method, but it does not defeat the counterexample. Even analytical compression can create different macro-verbs under different partitions. If later queries compare, count, rank, or narrate those macro-verbs, the partition becomes consequential and should be part of the receipt.

### R2 — some partitions are obvious from institutional boundaries

A legal corporation, agency, committee, contract party, or formally constituted coalition may supply a source-attested grouping.

**Response:** yes. In those cases the partition is partly source-defined rather than analyst-invented. But even then:

```text
formal membership
!=
functional coalition
!=
shared intent
```

The source-attested boundary should be carried as a stronger partition provenance class, not generalized to all emergent Names.

### R3 — the new rule already includes alternative decompositions

The addendum requires `alternative decompositions` in the higher-Name promotion record.

**Response:** this is direct counterevidence against claiming the method is naive. The pressure result is therefore a refinement, not a rejection: make the lift/partition itself a first-class receipt rather than treating alternatives as optional commentary.

## Nearest boring explanation

The nearest boring explanation for an apparently coherent higher-order network is often **analyst compression**:

1. a real set of micro-relations exists;
2. an analyst selects a bounded set of Names and verbs;
3. a grouping makes a repeated pattern visually or semantically legible;
4. the group acquires a convenient functional label;
5. repeated use of that label makes the grouping feel discovered rather than chosen.

No deception is required. This is ordinary abstraction pressure.

ALEX can preserve the gain without inheriting the illusion by receipting the grouping operation.

## PRESSURE verdict

- **Seed:** `STABLE LOWER PATTERNS MAY BECOME HIGHER NAMES.`
- **Literal verdict:** **malformed if “become” implies a partition-free or intrinsic macro-object; supported as a bounded candidate-compression rule after correction.**
- **What broke:** downward receipt reconstruction by itself does not make the higher-order Name unique or partition-invariant.
- **What survived:** recursive lifting is useful if the lift operation is attributable, reversible to micro-receipts, explicit about preservation target, and pressured against alternate decompositions.
- **Why it survived:** both the internal addendum guardrails and external formal literature already point toward non-reification and property-relative compression.
- **New prediction:** the same receipted micrograph will sometimes yield materially different higher-order Names/verbs under two defensible partitions. A lawful method should expose that delta rather than collapse it.
- **Residual weirdness:** some higher-level structures may prove unusually robust across independent partition rules and aperture rotations. That robustness would be an earned research fact, not proof of centralized intent.
- **Next discriminator:** freeze a two-partition hostile fixture and require the macro-layer to disclose which relations are partition-dependent.

## 9. Relation-only perturbation

When testing relational state, hold local carrier variables as constant as practicable and perturb only relations.

Template:

```text
X_A ~= X_B
G_A != G_B
observe P_A, P_B
```

Then ask:

```text
P_A != P_B ?
```

Silver-colloid example:

- hold particle size/shape/material/ligand distribution within tolerance;
- change interparticle spacing/topology;
- measure optical projection;
- independently witness actual topology.

This can establish that an observable depends on relation.

It cannot by itself establish memory, irreducibility, or computation.
